Based on the paper “Beyond Ranks: Measuring Brand Visibility in LLM Responses.” Research prototype. Scores are model-based estimates of how prominent a brand appears to readers of one specific answer, not a measurement. They are most reliable for recommendation-style answers with 3–8 brands.
